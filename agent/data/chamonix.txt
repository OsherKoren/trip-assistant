Complete 5-Day Chamonix Itinerary from Saint-Gervais-les-Bains
Perfect family plan (2 adults + teens 13/16/17) based in Saint-Gervais (20-min drive to Chamonix). All trails 6-9 km medium difficulty, nature-focused. Drive Aosta -> Saint-Gervais: 95 km via Mont Blanc Tunnel (55 EUR roundtrip).

Day 5 (July 11) — Chamonix Day 1: Arrival Day - Lake Passy + Gorges du Diosaz (Relaxed Teen Day)
9:00 AM - 11:00 AM: Drive Aosta -> Chamonix area (95 km, 1.5 hrs).
11:00 AM - 14:00 PM: Lac de Passy (15-min drive):
Beach volleyball, paddleboard rental (15 EUR/hr), 4 km lakeside loop
Karting du Mont Blanc (10-min heats, 90 EUR total)
Mont Blanc backdrop, ice cream at plage bar
15:00 - 17:00 PM: Gorges du Diosaz (10-min drive):
Suspended metal walkways over 50m gorge + waterfalls
Vertigo bridges, via ferrata intro (teens love thrill)
2 km loop (8 EUR/adult, 5 EUR/teens = 26 EUR total)

Day 6 (July 12) — Chamonix Day 2: Lac Blanc Loop via L'Index -> Cheserys Lakes (7.5 km)
Drive: Saint-Gervais -> Flegere/INDEX base Les Praz (25 min, P+R 10 EUR)
08:30 AM: Flegere gondola -> L'Index chairlift (35 EUR/adult RT, 18 EUR/teens = 129 EUR total)
UP (2.5 km, 1 hr): L'Index (2,590m) -> gravel path -> Combe des Crochues -> Lac Blanc (2,352m)
10:30 AM - 12:00 PM: Lunch Refuge Lac Blanc terrace (soup/croutes 15-20 EUR) + lake photos
LOOP RETURN (5 km, 3 hrs):
Lac Blanc back door -> "Lacs des Cheserys" wooden steps
Chalets des Cheserys (small lakes, marmots)
2 metal ladders over slabs -> Col des Cheserys junction
RIGHT "Flegere 1h30" -> Tete aux Vents meadows -> Flegere station
3:30 PM: Gondola down -> Saint-Gervais
Gear: Hiking boots, poles, 2L water/person.

Day 7 (July 13) — Chamonix Day 3: Grand Balcon Nord - Options A or B

Option A: Grand Balcon Nord (7.5 km)
9:00 AM: Arrive at Chamonix base station (Telepherique Aiguille du Midi)
9:20 AM: Board cable car -> Plan de l'Aiguille mid-station (2,317m)
9:30 AM: Side trip to Lac Bleu (15-min easy walk from station)
10:00 AM: Begin main hike toward Montenvers Mer de Glace
11:30 AM: Picnic lunch on trail (packed)
12:15 PM: Climb to Signal Forbes (2,198m): steep 30-min zig-zag stone steps
12:45 PM: Glacier views at Signal Forbes (Mer de Glace directly below)
1:15 PM: Descend toward Montenvers train station
2:00 PM: Montenvers Station (1,913m): gondola + stairs into ice caves (glacier)
3:30 PM: Montenvers cogwheel train back to Chamonix
4:00 PM: Back in Chamonix (full evening free)

Option B: Aiguille du Midi Summit + Grand Balcon Nord (early start)
7:15 AM: Arrive at Chamonix base station; scan pre-booked ticket at Telepherique Aiguille du Midi
7:30 AM: Board cable car; ascend to the peak
7:50 AM: Arrive at the Summit (3,842m): 2 hrs 10 min up top
  Walk to Le Pas dans le Vide (the glass box) while the line is still manageable
  Visit the museum, enjoy the early-morning panorama of Mont Blanc
10:00 AM: Cable car down to Plan de l'Aiguille mid-station (2,317m)
10:20 AM: Side trip to Lac Bleu (easy 15-min walk)
10:50 AM: Begin main hike: Grand Balcon Nord trail toward Montenvers
12:15 PM: Picnic lunch on trail (packed, open valley views)
1:15 PM: Climb to Signal Forbes (2,198m): steep 30-min zig-zag stone steps
1:45 PM: Glacier views at Signal Forbes (Mer de Glace directly below, stone cairns)
2:15 PM: Head down to Montenvers (winding path down the other side of the ridge)
3:00 PM: Montenvers Station (1,913m): 2.5-hr cushion before final train
  Gondola + stairs down to walk inside the Ice Caves carved into the glacier
5:00 PM: Board red Montenvers cogwheel train (sit on the right side for best views; clears before the last 5:30 PM train)
5:30 PM: Back in Chamonix (evening free)

Day 8 (July 14) — Chamonix Day 4: Emosson Dam + Zipline + Vieux-Emosson Hike (Switzerland Full Day)
7:30 AM - Drive Saint-Gervais -> Le Chatelard-Frontiere parking (45 min, free parking available)
8:15 AM - Arrive ticket office, buy 3-stage VerticAlp return tickets (CHF 161 total family: 2 adults CHF 46 each, 3 teens CHF 23 each)
8:30-10:00 AM - 3-STAGE TRANSPORT UP:
  Stage 1: Vallorcine-Chatelard cogwheel train (15 min steep climb)
  Stage 2: Chatelard -> Emosson Dam vertical funicular (world's steepest, 10 min)
  Stage 3: Panoramic train along dam crest to lake level (10 min, Mont Blanc views)
10:00-10:30 AM - Emosson Dam level (1,965m): Dam walk, engineering photos
Zipline Option (teens' choice):
10:30-11:00 AM - Tyrolienne EMOSSiONelle zipline across dam face (450m, 200m height)
  Pricing: CHF 22/adult x 2 + CHF 16/teen x 3 = CHF 92 total family
  Book at dam ticket counter after trains
Hiking Plan (everyone):
11:15 AM-1:15 PM - Hike Emosson Dam -> Cabane du Vieux-Emosson (6 km RT, 400m up, trail #12)
  Steady uphill through pastures -> alpine lake at 2,200m
  Marmots, glacier panorama, Mont Blanc massif
1:30-2:30 PM - Picnic lunch at Cabane du Vieux-Emosson refuge or lake shore (pack sandwiches)
2:45-4:30 PM - Return hike to main dam (easier downhill)
4:45 PM - Catch trains down (last descent ~5:15 PM)
6:00-6:30 PM - Drive back to France -> Chamonix
6:45-8:30 PM - Chamonix evening stroll (Arve River lights, gelato at Glacier Cafe, souvenir shops)
9:00 PM - Free bus to Saint-Gervais apartment
Total cost: CHF 161 (trains) + CHF 92 (zipline optional) = CHF 253 (~260 EUR)
Gear: Hiking boots, poles, swim towels (lake dip possible), 2L water/person, rain jackets, Swiss francs cash
Perfect teen day: Epic trains + adrenaline zip + alpine hike + Chamonix nightlife. Weatherproof engineering marvel!
